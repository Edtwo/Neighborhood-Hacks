## Filming at dusk

- **Light your face, not the sky.** Face the sunset, or use a small light or a car's interior light. A bright sky behind a dark face looks like a silhouette.
- **Record the phone screens separately** with the phone's own screen recorder, then cut them in. Filming a phone screen outdoors at dusk will glare and flicker.
- **Wind and ducks:** use a wired or clip-on mic if you have one. Otherwise turn your back to the wind.
- **Turn the CPR beat volume down** a bit so it doesn't drown out your voice.
- **The app works with no signal around Audubon Pond.** The neighborhood's map data is saved in the app, so a weak signal at the pond is fine. If the status line says "showing saved results", that's expected.
- Sunset in Allen in late October is around 6:40–6:50 PM (check the exact time for your filming day). Start filming about 20 minutes before so the dark shots at the end are really dark.

## Sources for the numbers

- CARES. *National Survival Report, 2013–2025, Non-Traumatic Etiology* (2026): 71.6% at home, 32.8% vs 10.2% survival, 2.1% bystander AED use.
- Wallace SK et al. Effect of time of day on prehospital care and outcomes after out-of-hospital cardiac arrest. *Circulation* 2013;127(15):1591–1596. [doi:10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.113.002058](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.113.002058). Night was 8 PM–8 AM; 30-day survival 8.56% vs 10.9%.
- Deakin CD et al. Underutilisation of public access defibrillation is related to retrieval distance and time-dependent availability. *Heart* 2018;104(16):1339–1343. [doi:10.1136/heartjnl-2018-312998](https://doi.org/10.1136/heartjnl-2018-312998). 34.3% of AEDs available at night.
- Note for Q&A: not every study finds a night effect. Vienna found none, crediting high bystander CPR rates at all hours (Schriefl C et al. *Resuscitation* 2019;142:61–68, [doi:10.1016/j.resuscitation.2019.07.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.resuscitation.2019.07.009)). That supports the point: what matters is bystanders acting fast.
- Neighborhood numbers: Heartline's saved OpenStreetMap data for Audubon Pond (`data/allen-audubon-pond.json`, made with `node scripts/snapshot.mjs`). "Open" for schools, churches and gyms uses typical hours for that kind of place, since their real AED hours aren't on the map.
