# Demo video script (target 3:30, limit 2–6 min)

Record the screen in Chrome DevTools phone mode (iPhone 12 Pro size), or on your real phone with screen recording. Speak over it. Replace every **[YOU]** with something true.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | You, or a shot of an AED cabinet on a wall in your neighborhood | "**[YOU]** one-sentence hook from your own experience, e.g. where you looked for an AED and couldn't find one." |
| 0:25–0:55 | `docs/evidence-aed-counts.md` table, then the CARES numbers on a slide | "In the national cardiac arrest registry, survival was about three times higher when a bystander used an AED: 33% versus 10%. But bystanders use one in only 2% of cases where they could. One reason: nobody can find them. The open map most apps use lists zero AEDs in Virginia Beach and zero in Dallas. **[YOU: your city's count]**." |
| 0:55–1:45 | Emergency tab → Find the nearest AED (use a place with results, e.g. demo "Seattle — Capitol Hill" or your own street) | "This is Heartline. In an emergency you get two buttons: call 911, and find the nearest AED. It checks which ones are open *right now*, estimates the round trip for someone running to get it, and tells you plainly: send someone, or start CPR. These gray ones are places that *usually* have an AED, like gyms and fire stations. They're labelled 'not confirmed', because in most cities the confirmed list is empty. 'Send to a helper' texts a neighbor the directions." |
| 1:45–2:15 | Start CPR coach (sound on), then flip to Español briefly | "The CPR coach keeps the beat at 110 a minute, with sound, vibration and voice, following American Heart Association hands-only CPR. It keeps your screen on, works offline, and speaks Spanish too." |
| 2:15–3:05 | Neighborhood gaps tab on **[YOU: your neighborhood]**: analyze, then switch to Saturday 2 AM, then point at the weekly graph and the numbered cabinet suggestions | "The second half is for neighbors before the emergency. Heartline colors every block by whether an AED can get there in four minutes, at any hour you pick. At **[YOU]** on a weekday it's X%. At 2 AM Saturday it drops to Y%, because most AEDs are locked inside schools and offices. It then picks the 24/7 places where an outdoor AED cabinet would cover the most homes, and prints it all as a one-page sheet you can bring to a city council." |
| 3:05–3:25 | Add an AED tab: drop a pin, fill the form, show the preview (don't need to send live) | "And if you spot an AED that isn't on the map, you add it in thirty seconds. It goes to OpenStreetMap, so every app that uses the open map learns about it, not just mine." |
| 3:25–3:40 | GitHub repo / About tab | "It's free, has no accounts, works on any phone, and the code is open. **[YOU] one line on what you'll do next, e.g. an AED hunt with your school club.**" |

Tips: rehearse once, keep the mouse still, and turn the CPR beat volume down a bit so it doesn't drown out your voice.
